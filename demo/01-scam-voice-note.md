# 1. The scam voice note

**Who is asking:** Chidi (Son) · **Amount:** 800 · **Payee email:** `scammer@example.com` (anything that is not Chidi's verified PayPal)

## English (read aloud, ~20 seconds)
> Mum, it's me. Please don't panic. My phone fell and broke so this is my new number. I'm in an accident. Please don't tell Dad. The lawyer will call you. Send eight hundred dollars right now, I'll explain later. Hurry, please.

## Nigerian Pidgin
> Mummy, na me. No fear. My phone don spoil, na my new number be this. I dey for accident. No tell Daddy. The lawyer go call you. Abeg send eight hundred dollar now now, I go explain later.

## What should happen
1. Risk **high**: payee mismatch, amount anomaly, urgency, secrecy, new number, third-party hand-off.
2. Ada approves the hold in PayPal. Status: *Waiting for the real person*.
3. Chidi's window shows the full-screen card: "Did you just ask Mum for $800?" He taps **No, that wasn't me**.
4. Authorization **voided**; Ada sees "We stopped this payment and your money is back."
